Microsoft has published official guidance on migration options for **Dynamics NAV, Dynamics GP, and Dynamics SL** customers, recalling lifecycle deadlines and pointing to **Dynamics 365 Business Central** as the destination platform.

## Key dates for Dynamics NAV

- Extended support for Dynamics NAV ends in **January 2028** (the final version was released in 2017).
- As of **April 30, 2031**, Annual Service Plan coverage ends, additional perpetual user licenses can no longer be purchased, and subscription licenses via Solution Provider Agreement (SPA) are no longer renewable; subscription use via SPLA is also expected to end.

## Why Business Central is positioned as the next step

According to the article, Business Central brings together finance, operations, inventory, sales, service, manufacturing, and projects on a single cloud platform, with **built-in AI capabilities**, continuous updates, and native integration with Microsoft 365, Teams, Outlook, Power Platform, and Azure.

## The migration path described by Microsoft

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Assess & Plan] --> B[Prepare the environment]
    B --> C[Move & validate data]
    C --> D[Enable users & optimize]
    D --> E[Go live with confidence]
```

- **Assess and plan**: evaluate the current ERP version, customizations, integrations, reporting, and business requirements.
- **Prepare the environment**: identify upgrade paths and determine how customizations will be handled.
- **Move and validate data**: migrate core business data, validate accuracy, and test business processes.
- **Enable users and optimize**: configure roles, reporting, and integrations while training users.
- **Go live with confidence**: transition to the cloud environment and establish a foundation for ongoing innovation.
