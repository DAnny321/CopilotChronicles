Microsoft's **2026 release wave 1** plans include a dedicated set of investments for **Dynamics 365 Business Central**, described as accelerating "the move to agentic ERP."

## What's coming

- Enhancements to **AI-powered agents that automate sales and purchase scenarios**.
- Heavy investment in **developer productivity for extensibility**, specifically:
  - Improved **AL (Advanced Language) testing**
  - Improved **debugging**
  - **Copilot extensibility**
  - **Agent design**

This positions Business Central's roadmap around two parallel tracks: automating more of the day-to-day sales/purchase workflow with agents, and making it easier for partners and ISVs to build and extend Copilot-powered capabilities on top of the platform.
