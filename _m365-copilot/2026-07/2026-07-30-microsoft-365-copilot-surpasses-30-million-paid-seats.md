Microsoft announced that **Microsoft 365 Copilot has surpassed 30 million paid seats**, with net seat adds **more than doubling quarter over quarter**, according to what was shared during the quarterly earnings call.

## What this milestone means

Microsoft frames this milestone not just as an adoption metric, but as an indicator of a broader shift in how work gets done at organizations that have adopted Copilot, a theme the company calls "AI momentum" in the official post.

The figure comes alongside a series of announcements about the evolution of Microsoft 365 Copilot throughout 2026, including the general availability of **Copilot Cowork** (June 16, 2026) and the Copilot app redesign (May 28, 2026), both referenced as part of the same growth trajectory.
