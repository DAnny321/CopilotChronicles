As part of the **2026 release wave 1** plans, Microsoft detailed how role-based agents in **Microsoft 365 Copilot** are evolving into "intelligent daily command centers" for specific job roles.

## Sales Agent

Becomes the seller's daily command center with richer **Sales Chat** and **Sales Home** experiences across desktop and mobile, including streamlined access to deal and account insights through configurable record summaries, contextual support in **Outlook** and **Teams**, and improved email and meeting intelligence. New governance and extensibility controls help organizations scale AI responsibly.

## Finance Agent

Continues expanding support for common finance tasks such as **reconciliation, variance analysis, and data preparation in Excel**, as well as customer communications in Outlook — helping finance teams investigate issues faster and spend less time manually preparing or reconciling data.
