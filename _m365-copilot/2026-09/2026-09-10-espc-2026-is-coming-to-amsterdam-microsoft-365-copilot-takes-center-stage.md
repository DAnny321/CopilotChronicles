Microsoft has confirmed it will be a **headline sponsor of ESPC 2026**, the Microsoft 365 and AI Conference, taking place **November 30 – December 3, 2026** at the RAI in Amsterdam.

## What's planned

- **Opening keynote** by Jeff Teper, Executive Vice President, Microsoft Apps and Agents, on the future of AI-powered productivity and what's next for Microsoft 365 Copilot
- **More than 100 expert speakers**, **128 sessions**, and **11 in-depth tutorials** across nine content tracks
- Dedicated content on **Microsoft Copilot Studio**, exploring how organizations move "beyond prompts and into business transformation" with custom AI agents, workflow automation, and enterprise data connections

## Why it matters for Microsoft 365 Copilot

The event continues themes from ESPC 2025, where Microsoft shared how organizations are adopting Copilot, reimagining business processes, and building new experiences through AI-powered agents. ESPC 2026 sessions will focus on accelerating Copilot adoption, scaling AI across the organization, and strengthening governance and security around agentic deployments.
